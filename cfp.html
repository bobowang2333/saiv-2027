Call for Papers

October 31 – November 5, 2027

SAIV 2027 invites regular paper submissions on AI verification. The list of topics falling under the scope of SAIV 2027 is provided below. Papers should be original work that have not been published at or are under submission to another peer-reviewed venue; see the dual submission policy below. We also invite presentations of recently published or ongoing work at our AI Safety Research Highlights event.

Subject Areas

SAIV 2027 is a conference dedicated to the advancement of AI with rigorous formal guarantees. We invite submissions from all researchers, irrespective of area boundaries, who use logical and mathematical methods to establish or assess the safety, alignment, security, privacy, and interpretability of AI, as well as work that uses AI to advance formal reasoning itself. By AI, we refer not only to learned neural models but also to the digital agents and physical robots powered by them. Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Formal specifications for AI
  • Neuro-symbolic architectures with formal guarantees
  • Formal methods for AI, including verification, testing, falsification, probabilistic analysis, and runtime monitoring:
    • Verifying neural models, from small edge models to foundation and world models
    • Verifying agents and physical robots
    • Formal explainability and interpretability
  • AI for formal methods:
    • Autoformalization
    • Formal theorem proving
    • Verified code generation and translation

Dual Submission Policy

A submitted paper should be original work that has not been published and is not under submission to another peer-reviewed venue. However, works that have appeared on archival websites (such as arXiv) or that have been presented at venues that do not have publication proceedings (such as workshops without proceedings) can be submitted to SAIV 2027. The dual submission policy is enforced during the whole reviewing process period. Submission of papers to archival repositories such as arXiv is allowed during the review process.

AI Use Policy

The use of AI in submission preparation is allowed, but should be disclosed in the paper under the “AI Use Statement”, which does not count towards the page limit. However, authors remain fully responsible for the content of their papers. Any instances of plagiarism, non-existent references, and other cases of LLM hallucinations or bloat will lead to desk rejection.

AI Safety Research Highlights

Presentations of recently published or ongoing work are welcome. Event details and submission instructions will be announced.
